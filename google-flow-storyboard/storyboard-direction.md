# AI Receptionist Reel — “The Concierge Orbit”

## Reference synthesis

- **Instagram reference 1:** high-retention 2.5D montage, foreground objects crossing the lens, aggressive scale changes, cropped keyword typography, and object-led transitions.
- **Instagram reference 2:** restrained editorial pacing, circular motion paths, conceptual match cuts, sculptural metaphors, and phrase-by-phrase type reveals.
- **Pinterest 1:** believable AR-style notifications composited into a real environment, shallow depth of field, and interaction between physical props and floating UI.
- **Pinterest 2:** very bold color blocking, oversized cropped typography, diagonal phone arrays, rapid hard resets, and graphic collage transitions.
- **Pinterest 3:** tactile gradient lighting, cursor-driven selections, rounded dimensional labels, glossy spheres, and energetic statistic reveals.
- **Pinterest 4:** minimal metaphor design, clock and hourglass imagery, sweeping arcs, generous negative space, and selective highlight color.
- **Pinterest 5:** dark premium glass UI, blue-edge lighting, controlled 3D camera drift, reflective cards, luminous spheres, and UI elements that physically assemble.

The storyboard borrows the **motion principles**, not the references' layouts or recognizable sequences.

## Original concept

**The Concierge Orbit:** an incoming call is represented by a physical glass orb. It begins burgundy while unanswered, travels through the medspa environment, is captured by an AI concierge mechanism, turns emerald, and constructs the demonstration interface. The orb supplies a consistent object, camera target, lighting cue, and transition device across all eight scenes.

Visual language: warm travertine, warm ivory paper, smoked glass, brushed forest-green metal, emerald glass, and minimal burgundy. Cinematic macro product photography meets editorial SaaS interface design. Avoid robots, AI brains, neon cyberpunk, holograms, particles, and uncontrolled geometry morphing.

Target running time: approximately **36 seconds**, adjustable to the real Retell recording.

## Waveform direction

- Customer waveform: soft ivory/light gray, 12–18 understated vertical bars.
- AI receptionist waveform: emerald `#008C69`, same bar count and geometry.
- Active speaker: bars interpolate from 25% to 100% height with fast attack and gentle release; no random flicker.
- Inactive speaker: 20–25% height and 35% opacity.
- Switch speakers only on verified turn boundaries from the real recording.
- Avoid glow beyond a subtle 2–4 px edge bloom. Do not use a holographic or audio-reactive particle field.
- Generate the environment and glass panel in Flow; composite the deterministic waveforms and captions afterward for accuracy.

## Flow consistency controls

Use the ending keyframe of each scene as the starting reference image for the next. Reuse the same phone, orb, concierge mechanism, medspa room, travertine texture, lens height, and lighting direction. Generate plates without long text where possible; add exact typography and verified transcript as overlays to prevent distorted glyphs.
