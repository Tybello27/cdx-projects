# AI Receptionist Reel — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

A premium vertical SaaS advertisement with the restraint of an Apple product film and the warmth of a luxury medspa. Editorial scale, precise interface details, soft dimensional shadows, generous negative space, and purposeful motion make the system feel trustworthy rather than futuristic-for-its-own-sake. Code-generated medspa illustration avoids licensing ambiguity while retaining a polished campaign look.

## Colors

- Warm ivory `#F5F0E9` — primary light canvas and copy on dark scenes
- Forest green `#17251F` — dark canvas, interface shells, and primary typography
- Emerald `#008C69` — active call state, waveform, highlights, and CTA
- Burgundy `#782A3B` — rare missed-call accent only
- Soft sage `#C7D6CD` — secondary interface and illustration tone

## Typography

- Manrope, 300/500/700/800 — all display, caption, interface, and data registers
- Display tracking: `-0.045em`; body tracking: `-0.01em`
- Headlines: 76–132 px; captions: 46–54 px; interface labels: 24–34 px

## Motion Language

- Confident `expo.out` and `power3.out` entrances for product UI
- Gentle `sine.inOut` ambient motion for wellness scenes
- Push slides for related ideas, a zoom-through for the solution reveal, and a luxe blur crossfade into the CTA
- No infinite animation loops; all motion is seekable and bounded to scene duration

## Safe Areas

- Keep critical copy below 170 px from the top
- Keep captions and CTA above 300 px from the bottom
- Maintain at least 72 px horizontal inset

## What NOT to Do

- No robots, AI brains, neon grids, or particle fields
- No uncontrolled bouncing, random movement, or decorative camera shake
- No unverified booking confirmation or fabricated call dialogue
- No watermarked or ambiguously licensed stock media
- No burgundy except for the missed-call problem state
